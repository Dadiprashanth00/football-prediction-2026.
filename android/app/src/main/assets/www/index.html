Predictions are statistical estimates, never guaranteed outcomes. Probabilities come from a Poisson / Dixon–Coles goal model fed by recent form, home and away splits, opponent strength and head-to-head records. Football is uncertain: even a 70% pick loses three times in ten. If you bet, do so legally, only if you are 18+ (or the legal age where you live), and only with money you can afford to lose. Club names are used for identification only; badges shown are generic monograms.

Data source

The default source is free real data: official 2026/27 fixtures, results and live scores for all eight competitions, refreshed every 30 minutes (live scores every minute while matches are on). You can also use your own football-data.org or API-Football key, or the offline demo season. The claude.ai preview page blocks outside connections, so it always shows the demo.
